How We Built It

Phase 1: Listen

"We were very systematic around how we worked internally with teams and understood their requirements."

Phase 2: Standardise

Narrowed languages. Created reusable modules. Built the integrated platform as a product with weekly releases.

Phase 3: Enable

Ran bootcamps to train engineers. Created champions in every team. Made adoption easy and rewarding.

Phase 4: Scale

Innersource model. Decentralised governance. Any team can contribute back. The platform evolves with the org.

Lessons Learned

01

Let teams run first

You NEED the chaotic cloud adoption phase. Teams need to feel the pain of inconsistency before they'll embrace standardisation.

02

Build a product, not a project

Weekly releases. Treat the platform like a product your teams are the customers of. They inherit value every time they deploy.

03

Compliance as a feature

In a regulated org, make security invisible. Teams shouldn't spend effort on compliance - they should GET it for free.

04

Decentralise to scale

Innersource prevents the platform team from becoming a bottleneck. Champions everywhere. Anyone can contribute.

Speaker Notes

(3 min): These are the takeaways people will remember. Be candid and conversational.

Lesson 1: "I know this sounds counterintuitive. But if you try to standardise too early, you get resistance. People need to feel the pain."

Lesson 2: "Projects end. Products evolve. We release weekly - that cadence keeps us accountable."

Lesson 3: "Our compliance team went from being worried to being our biggest champions. They get evidence automatically."

Lesson 4: "GitHub innersource model. Curated orgs. Pull requests reviewed by champions in delivery teams, not a central team."
